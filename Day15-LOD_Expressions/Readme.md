# Day 15 – Tableau LOD Expressions

## 📌 Overview
Today, I learned about Level of Detail (LOD) expressions in Tableau and explored how to perform calculations at different levels of granularity.

## 📚 Topics Covered

### 1. FIXED LOD
- Calculated total sales across the entire dataset.
- Calculated total sales at the Category level.
- Understood how FIXED maintains calculations independently of dimensions in the visualization.

### 2. EXCLUDE LOD
- Excluded Category from the calculation.
- Excluded Sub-Category from the calculation.
- Understood how EXCLUDE changes the level of detail relative to the dimensions in the view.

## 💻 Practical Implementation

Created calculated fields:
- `Total_Sales_All`
- `Total_Sales_Category`
- `Total_Sales_exclude`
- `Sales_Binder`
- `Total_sales_subcategory`
- `diff_in_sales`

## 🎯 Key Learnings
- Understood the purpose of LOD expressions.
- Learned the difference between FIXED and EXCLUDE.
- Practised creating calculated fields.
- Explored how LOD expressions help analyse sales at different levels of detail.
